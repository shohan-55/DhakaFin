Privacy & information

DhakaFin is a public informational website. We do not ask you to submit confidential tax records through this website. When you use the contact form, your own email application prepares the message; nothing is stored by the page. If you send an email, it is processed by the relevant email providers. Hosting providers may process standard technical access logs. Avoid including sensitive documents in an initial enquiry. For privacy questions, email hello.sohan@outlook.com.

Legal and policy pages are design drafts. Review and approve final content before publication.
